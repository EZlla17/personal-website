## Design Context

### Users
- **Who**: Design recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators in the tech and AI space.
- **Context**: Reviewing a portfolio to understand Ella's design process, specifically her expertise in SaaS and AI design innovation.
- **Job to be done**: quickly evaluate Ella's skills, experience (e.g., Alibaba), and design sensibility.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Professional, forward-thinking, and precise.
- **Tone**: Tech-forward, refined, and minimalist.
- **3-word personality**: Intellectual, Modern, Precise.
- **Emotional goals**: Trust, innovation, and "wow" factor through subtle details.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: "AI Product Styling" - clean, white background, high-end typography, subtle motion.
- **References**: "Impeccable" website (for clean, premium feel).
- **Theme**: Light mode (oklch paper white).
- **Anti-references**: Cluttered layouts, generic "AI slop" gradients, overused sans-serif pairs (like Inter).

### Design Principles
1. **Refined Typography**: Use `Cormorant Garamond` for an editorial, sophisticated feel and `Instrument Sans` for clarity and modernism.
2. **Subtle Motion**: Incorporate micro-animations and staggered reveals to make the interface feel alive but not distracting.
3. **Generous Whitespace**: Let the content breathe to highlight the premium nature of the work.
4. **Precision in Detail**: Use fine lines, subtle shadows, and uniform spacing to convey a high level of craft.
