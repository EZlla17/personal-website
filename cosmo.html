02

Tagging Experience

Design Challenge

How should users add and personalize tags for their resources in an efficient way?

Before

  • One missed comma creates messy, inconsistent tags.
  • Without suggestions, users wonder what tags exist.
  • Users could accidentally create duplicates.
  • Limited visual feedback.

After

Implemented automatic tag tokenization (visual chips) with autocomplete suggestions, preventing duplicates and formatting errors.
